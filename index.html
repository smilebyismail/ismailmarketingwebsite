How an engagement runs

The audit comes first and stands on its own. You can act on it yourself, hand it to your team, or hire me to do the work.

Book your audit

  1. Audit

    I go through your ad accounts, website, tracking and search presence, and list what is leaking money or enquiries.

  2. Plan

    You get priorities in order, with what each fix should be worth, so you can decide what to do first.

  3. Launch and fix

    I set up campaigns, pages and tracking, and test that every enquiry is counted before spending scales.

  4. Report and improve

    A plain monthly report on enquiries and cost per enquiry, and the changes I am making next.

Questions before you book

What does Ismail Marketing do?

I run paid ads on Google, Meta, LinkedIn and TikTok, handle SEO and local SEO, set up Google Analytics 4 and Search Console, and build WhatsApp and email automation. One person looks after the full path from a search or ad to an enquiry.

What does the audit include?

I review your ad accounts, website, tracking and search presence, then send you a short list of what to fix first and what it should be worth. You can use it with me or with someone else.

Which businesses do you work with?

Service businesses, clinics, manufacturers, schools, real estate and online stores, mainly in India. If you can describe your customer and what an enquiry is worth, I can probably help.

How do you measure results?

By enquiries and cost per enquiry, tracked through GA4, your ad platforms and a simple report. Clicks and impressions are shown, but they are not the goal.

Do I sign a long contract?

Contract length and terms are agreed after the audit, based on the work involved. Ask me in the audit call.

Can you work alongside my in-house team or developer?

Yes. I often handle ads and tracking while your team handles the website or sales follow-up.
