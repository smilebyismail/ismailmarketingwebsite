Questions before you book

What do you do?

I run ads on Google, Meta, LinkedIn and TikTok. I also do SEO, set up Google Analytics and Search Console, and build WhatsApp and email automation. One person looks after the whole path from ad or search to lead.

What is in the free audit?

I check your ad accounts, website, tracking and Google presence. Then I send a short list of what to fix first and what it could be worth. You can use it with me or with anyone else.

Who do you work with?

Service businesses, clinics, factories, schools, real estate and online shops, mostly in India. If you know your customer and what a lead is worth, I can probably help.

How do I know it is working?

I track leads and cost per lead using GA4 and your ad accounts. You get a simple report each month. I show clicks too, but leads matter most.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

We agree the terms after the audit, based on the work needed. Ask me during the audit call.

Can you work with my team or developer?

Yes. Often I handle ads and tracking while your team handles the website or sales calls.
